# LOOP - Consumer Carbon Loop App

## 📋 Project Overview

**LOOP** is a personal carbon footprint tracking application built for the HackOut'26 hackathon. The name "LOOP" represents the circular feedback loop between consumer behavior and environmental impact - helping users see, understand, and close the loop on their carbon emissions.

### The Problem We're Solving

1. **Most people have no idea where their personal carbon comes from** - They know "cars are bad" but don't realize their food delivery habit or fast fashion shopping creates significant emissions
2. **Existing tools require tedious manual entry** - Users give up after a few days
3. **Generic advice doesn't help** - "Take public transport" is useless if you live where there isn't any
4. **Individual guilt without social context fails** - People need peer comparison and community to change behavior

### Our Solution

LOOP automatically parses your bank/UPI transaction CSV and calculates your carbon footprint using emission factors based on merchant categories. No manual entry. Just upload and see your impact.

## 🌍 What is Carbon Footprint?

### Understanding Carbon Emissions

A **carbon footprint** is the total amount of greenhouse gases (primarily CO₂) generated by our actions. Everything we buy, eat, use, and do has an associated carbon cost.

### How Activities Create Carbon

| Activity | How It Creates CO₂ |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Food Delivery** | Packaging production, delivery vehicle fuel, restaurant energy use |
| **Ride Hailing** | Vehicle fuel combustion, manufacturing emissions |
| **Electricity** | Power plant emissions (India's grid is ~70% coal-based) |
| **Flights** | Jet fuel combustion at high altitude (worse impact) |
| **Fashion** | Cotton farming, synthetic fabric production, global shipping |
| **Electronics** | Mining rare earth metals, manufacturing, global shipping |
| **Streaming** | Data center electricity, network infrastructure |

### Carbon Measurement

- **Unit**: kg CO₂e (kilograms of CO₂ equivalent)
- **"Equivalent"** means we convert other greenhouse gases (methane, nitrous oxide) to their CO₂ warming equivalent
- **India Average**: ~1.9 tonnes CO₂ per person per year (consumption-based)
- **Global Average**: ~4.7 tonnes CO₂ per person per year

## 📊 How LOOP Calculates Your Carbon

### Emission Factors

We use emission factors derived from the **GHG Protocol** and **DEFRA** (UK government) standards, adapted for Indian consumption patterns:

```
Category              Factor (kg CO₂ per ₹1 spent)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Flights               0.0035  (highest)
Fuel                  0.0025
Electronics           0.0015
Ride Hailing          0.0012
Electricity           0.00082
Food Delivery         0.0008
Hotels                0.0007
Fashion/Shopping      0.0006
Dining Out            0.0004
Other                 0.0004
Grocery               0.0003
Gym/Wellness          0.0002
Public Transport      0.00015 (lowest)
Streaming             0.00005
```

### The Math

```
CO₂ (kg) = Amount Spent (₹) × Emission Factor
```

Example:
- ₹500 on Swiggy = 500 × 0.0008 = **0.4 kg CO₂**
- ₹2000 Ola ride = 2000 × 0.0012 = **2.4 kg CO₂**
- ₹5000 flight booking = 5000 × 0.0035 = **17.5 kg CO₂**

### Why Spending-Based?

We calculate carbon from spending rather than precise activity data because:
1. **Transaction data is readily available** (bank CSVs, UPI exports)
2. **Higher spending generally correlates with higher carbon** (more deliveries, longer rides, bigger purchases)
3. **It captures the full supply chain** - not just direct emissions

## 🎯 The Loop Score (0-1000)

### What It Means

Your Loop Score is a relative measure of your carbon efficiency compared to others in your city.

| Score Range | Personality | What It Means |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| 801-1000 | **Green Pioneer** | Leading by example, measurably low footprint |
| 651-800 | **Conscious Optimizer** | Sustainability is part of your lifestyle |
| 501-650 | **Mindful Consumer** | Above average, making conscious choices |
| 351-500 | **Convenience Consumer** | Aware but not consistent |
| 201-350 | **Habitual Spender** | Convenient but carbon-heavy habits |
| 0-200 | **Carbon Heavy** | Significantly above average footprint |

### How It's Calculated

```python
# Uses exponential decay relative to city average
ratio = your_co2 / city_average_co2
score = 1000 × e^(-0.29 × ratio)
```

This formula ensures:
- **Zero footprint** → ~1000 points
- **Half of city average** → ~870 points (Green Pioneer)
- **At city average** → ~750 points (Mindful Consumer)
- **2× city average** → ~560 points (Convenience Consumer)
- **5× city average** → ~230 points (Carbon Heavy)

```
1. User uploads CSV
   ↓
2. CSV Parser extracts: merchant, amount, date
   ↓
3. AI Classifier categorizes each merchant
   ↓
4. Emission factors calculate CO₂ per transaction
   ↓
5. Scoring calculates Loop Score & Personality
   ↓
6. Results displayed on Dashboard
```

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. **Automatic CSV Parsing**
- Supports bank statements and UPI exports
- Auto-detects columns (merchant, amount, date)
- Handles various date formats and currencies

### 2. **AI Transaction Classification**
- Uses LLM (via OpenRouter) for merchant categorization
- Rule-based fallback for common merchants
- 14 spending categories

### 3. **Manual Expense Logger**
- Quick Add buttons for common expenses:
  - Food Delivery ₹300
  - Cab Ride ₹200
  - Petrol ₹500
  - Chai ₹50
  - Metro ₹40
  - Grocery ₹500
  - Electricity ₹1500
  - Shopping ₹1000
- Custom form for any expense

### 4. **Carbon Personality System**
- 6 distinct personas based on score
- Evolves as behavior changes
- Each has unique messaging and color

### 5. **Impact Equivalents**
- 12 India-relevant comparisons
- Auto-selects 6 most impactful to display
- Makes abstract numbers tangible

### 6. **Carbon Calendar**
- GitHub-style heatmap
- Daily CO₂ intensity visualization
- Darker = higher carbon day

### 7. **City Rankings**
- Percentile rank among city users
- Drives local competition

### 8. **AI Carbon Story**
- Weekly AI-written narrative
- Personalized to your data
- Honest, actionable, not preachy

### 9. **Action Plan**
- AI-generated reduction suggestions
- Filtered by your city
- Prioritized by impact

### 10. **Local Action Map**
- Recycling centers nearby
- E-waste drop-offs
- Repair cafes
- Filtered by what you bought

### 11. **Social Circles**
- Create/join friend groups
- Leaderboard competition
- City-wide auto-circles

### 12. **Milestones & Gamification**
- 9 achievement badges
- Progress tracking
- Celebration modals

### 13. **Dark/Light Mode**
- System preference detection
- Persistent user choice
- Smooth transitions

### 14. **Duplicate Detection**
- SHA-256 hash of CSV contents
- Prevents gaming by re-uploading same file
